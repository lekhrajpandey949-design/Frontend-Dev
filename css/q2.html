Welcome to Mathura

The birthplace of Lord Krishna — where spirituality meets culture.

Culture & Festivals

Mathura is known for its colorful Holi celebrations and Janmashtami festivities. Every street here sings with devotion, art, and vibrant energy.

Must-Visit Places

  • Banke Bihari Temple
  • Vishram Ghat
  • Dwarkadhish Temple

Best Time to Visit

October to March offers pleasant weather. Don’t miss the spiritual aarti at sunset — it’s a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
