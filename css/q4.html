The Future of Artificial Intelligence

By Lekhraj Pandey | October 2025

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the world around us in ways we never imagined.

From self-driving cars to personalized assistants, AI continues to make our lives easier and more efficient.

However, experts believe that we must be cautious about the ethical implications of this technology.

“The real question is not whether machines think but whether men do.” — B.F. Skinner

With ongoing research, AI could soon surpass human intelligence in specific areas, making it both an opportunity and a challenge.

As AI evolves, humans must focus on collaboration, innovation, and ethical use to ensure a balanced and sustainable future.
